About Us

MuRAL Lab – Multi-Robot Autonomy and Learning Lab focuses on advancing the theory and practice of multi-agent coordination and cooperation for autonomous robotic systems. The lab’s research centers on reinforcement learning and related learning-based methods for multi-robot autonomy, enabling teams of robots to perceive, navigate, and make decisions collectively in complex, uncertain environments. MuRAL Lab emphasizes real-world applications in environmental monitoring, exploration, navigation, and perception, with a particular focus on challenging domains such as underwater, polar, aerial, and unstructured terrestrial environments. Through a combination of algorithmic development, simulation, and field-ready robotic systems, the lab aims to build robust, scalable autonomy for real-world multi-robot deployments.

Projects

  • Policy Search for Active Sampling: Adaptive sampling using non‑myopic reinforcement learning to plan informative and efficient data collection paths.
  • Multirobot Water Sampling: A heterogeneous fleet of surface vehicles used for coordinated environmental monitoring in aquatic environments.
  • Multi-Scale Trajectory Planning: Energy-efficient sampling for spatial mapping using variable-resolution coverage strategies.
